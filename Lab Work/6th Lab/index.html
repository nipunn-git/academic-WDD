About Anviksha: The Epoch

Anviksha is a creative, technology-driven event designed to bring students together through engaging challenges, interactive experiences, and unconventional problem-solving. It focuses on pushing participants beyond conventional thinking, combining innovation, strategy, creativity, and a bit of chaos to create an experience that is both competitive and memorable.

This year we are excited to present "Anviksha: The Epoch," a unique event that promises to challenge your intellect and creativity.

The main theme this year revolves around the "Evolution of Technology" especially in gaming and how it has transformed the way we interact with the world. Participants will have the opportunity to explore the latest advancements in technology, engage in hands-on activities, and showcase their skills in a variety of competitions.
