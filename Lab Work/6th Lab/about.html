About Anviksha: The Epoch

Our Vision

Anviksha is a creative, technology-driven event designed to bring students together through engaging challenges, interactive experiences, and unconventional problem-solving. It focuses on pushing participants beyond conventional thinking, combining innovation, strategy, creativity, and enthusiasm to create an experience that is both competitive and memorable.

Event Theme: Evolution of Technology

This year's theme, "Anviksha: The Epoch," explores the profound impact of technological evolution, especially in gaming. The main theme revolves around the "Evolution of Technology" and how it has transformed the way we interact with the world.

What We Offer

  • Engaging technical and creative challenges
  • Opportunities to showcase your skills
  • Networking with like-minded students
  • Hands-on experience with latest technologies
  • Exciting prizes and recognition

Why Participate?

Learn: Gain insights from industry experts and fellow innovators.

Compete: Test your skills in exciting challenges with amazing prizes.

Network: Connect with like-minded individuals and potential collaborators.

Showcase: Display your talent and impress peers.
